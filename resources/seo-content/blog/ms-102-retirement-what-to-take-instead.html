Microsoft is retiring the MS-102 Microsoft 365 Administrator exam. If you are studying for it, have booked it, or hold the certification it leads to, this post explains what Microsoft has said, what it means for you, and which exam Microsoft points to as the newer option.

Dates and names come from Microsoft's MS-102 study guide and its Microsoft Credentials roundup of June 2026 (updated August 14, 2026), and were checked on November 5, 2026. Plans like this can change, so check Microsoft Learn before you decide.

What Microsoft has announced

The MS-102 study guide carries a warning at the top: the exam will retire on November 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM Central Standard Time. In Microsoft's credentials roundup, the exam sits in a table of retiring certifications, listed with the Microsoft 365 Certified: Administrator Expert certification, a retirement of November 2026, and a recommended new certification: Microsoft 365 Certified: AI Services Administrator Associate, which uses exam AB-650.

The roundup also sets out three rules that apply to every retiring certification in the table:

  • If you have already earned the certification, it remains valid until it expires.
  • You can still earn the certification before its retirement date.
  • If you are just beginning to prepare for the exam, consider focusing on a newer option.

The MS-102 study guide I checked names the retirement date but does not name a replacement exam in its text. The replacement named in the roundup is AB-650.

What it means for you

If you are about to start studying

Microsoft's own advice is to consider the newer option. With the retirement date less than a month away, a new learner is unlikely to prepare, book and pass MS-102 comfortably in time. Read the AB-650 guide and the official study guide first.

If you are already studying and have a date booked

You can still take MS-102 before November 30, 2026. Microsoft's roundup says you can still earn the certification before its retirement date, so a booked exam before that date still counts. Do not reschedule it for a date after the retirement date. Check your booking details on Microsoft Learn and confirm any questions with Microsoft's certification support.

If you already passed MS-102

Your certification stays valid until it expires. Associate and expert certifications are renewed each year with a free online assessment, as long as one is still offered for that certification, so check your Microsoft Learn profile for renewal availability. Our post on how renewal works explains the process.

MS-102 and AB-650: what each one measures

Microsoft's study guides show how different the two exams are.

MS-102: Microsoft 365 AdministratorAB-650: Administering Microsoft 365 and AI Services
Deploy and manage a Microsoft 365 tenant (25–30%)Configure and manage Microsoft 365 tenants and workloads (20–25%)
Implement and manage Microsoft Entra identity and access (25–30%)Govern and secure Microsoft 365 tenants and workloads (40–45%)
Manage security and threats by using Microsoft Defender XDR (30–35%)Manage and secure AI services in Microsoft 365 (35–40%)
Manage compliance by using Microsoft Purview (10–15%)(Purview data protection sits inside the governance and security domain)

Three things stand out.

  1. Tenant and identity skills carry over. Managing a Microsoft 365 tenant, users, groups, Microsoft Entra authentication and access, and Microsoft Purview data protection all appear in AB-650's outline.
  2. AI services are new. More than a third of AB-650 is about enabling and managing Microsoft 365 Copilot, implementing and managing agents, securing and governing agents, and monitoring AI services. If you have only worked in classic administration, this is the area to learn.
  3. The Defender XDR domain does not map directly. MS-102 gave a full domain to Defender XDR, Defender for Office 365, Defender for Endpoint and Defender for Cloud Apps. AB-650 folds security into its governance domain and does not describe the same Defender detail. If those skills matter to your job, look at the SC-200 security operations exam as well.

This comparison is from the outlines. Read both study guides before you decide, because Microsoft updates them.

Other exams that cover parts of the same ground

MS-102 spans a lot of ground, so parts of it are covered by other current exams. These are not replacements, and Microsoft has not named them as such. They are useful if you want to keep building skills in one area:

  • MD-102: managing and securing endpoints with Intune.
  • SC-300: identity and access administration.
  • SC-401: administering information security in Microsoft 365, which covers Microsoft Purview.
  • MS-700: managing Microsoft Teams.
  • AB-900: Microsoft 365 Copilot and Agent Administration fundamentals, a gentler way into the AI side.

A decision guide

  • New to Microsoft 365 administration: start with the AB-650 material. Spend extra time on the AI services domain, and use the free practice questions to see what is new.
  • Experienced administrator with the exam booked before November 30: prepare for MS-102 as planned and keep your booking date.
  • Experienced administrator with no booking: time is short. Weigh whether you can pass MS-102 before the deadline against starting AB-650, and note that the AB-650 outline weighs governance and AI heavily.
  • Already certified: you do not need to do anything now. Keep an eye on your profile for renewal.

How to prepare if you choose MS-102

Use the MS-102 guide for the skills and weightings. Focus first on the two heaviest domains, security and threats with Defender XDR (30–35%) and the tenant and identity domains (each 25–30%). Allow time for hands-on practice, and use practice questions to find gaps. Because the exam retires, there is no benefit in studying topics that were removed from the latest outline: check the date on the study guide.

The retirement date is a hard deadline, so work backwards from it. This is a planning sketch, not Microsoft's schedule:

  • Now (early November): read both study guides, check your Microsoft Learn profile for any booking, and decide which exam fits.
  • If you keep MS-102: aim to take the exam by the middle of the month, so that a failed attempt does not leave you without a second chance before November 30.
  • If you move to AB-650: plan a normal preparation period, starting with the governance and AI services domains, which carry most of the weight.
  • After November 30, 2026: MS-102 can no longer be taken. Any certification you already earned stays valid until it expires.

Frequently asked questions

When exactly does MS-102 retire?

According to Microsoft's study guide, on November 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM Central Standard Time.

Which exam does Microsoft recommend instead?

Microsoft's credentials roundup lists the Microsoft 365 Certified: AI Services Administrator Associate certification, which uses exam AB-650, as the recommended new certification alongside the retiring Administrator Expert certification and MS-102.

Will my MS-102 certification be removed?

Microsoft says that if you have already earned a certification it remains valid until it expires.

Is AB-650 the same level as MS-102?

The roundup names an Associate certification as the recommended new option. We have not compared the level of difficulty, and the two outlines are quite different, so read both study guides.

Where can I practise?

Try the free practice questions for MS-102 and AB-650, and read each exam's guide for the skills, weightings and the date Microsoft last updated the outline.
