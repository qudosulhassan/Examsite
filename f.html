Frequently asked questions about AzureExamsHub

How the practice questions, PDF guides and test engine work, how we use Microsoft's official study guides, and how to get help.

AzureExamsHub is an independent exam-preparation site for Microsoft certification exams. Each exam has a guide that follows Microsoft's official skills outline, a page of free practice questions with explanations, and plans that add a larger question bank, a timed online test engine and a PDF study guide.

No. AzureExamsHub is not produced, endorsed or approved by Microsoft. Exam names and certification titles belong to Microsoft. Our material is for study and practice and is not official exam content.

Yes. Open the free practice questions page of any exam, answer the questions, check each answer and read the explanation. You do not need an account or a card.

The test engine runs in your browser. You can practise question by question with instant feedback and an explanation, or switch to a timed exam mode that scores you at the end. Afterwards you can review every answer to find the topics that need more work.

The PDF is a printable study guide you can read offline on any device. The test engine is online and interactive: it scores you, times you and lets you review your answers. The bundle gives you both for one exam.

No. We do not publish or sell real exam questions or braindumps, and we encourage you not to use them: Microsoft prohibits sharing real exam content, and memorised answers do not prove you can do the job. Our practice questions help you test your understanding of the skills an exam measures.

Each guide is built from the exam's official study guide on Microsoft Learn: the domains, their weightings, the listed skills and the date Microsoft last updated the outline. We add a study plan and FAQs to help you use that outline, and every guide links back to the official page.

Every plan includes three months of updates from the day you buy, and you can add six or twelve months. Each exam page shows when its question bank was last updated, and the guide shows the date of the official skills outline it follows. Microsoft changes and retires exams from time to time, so check the official study guide before you book.

Access is added to your account as soon as your order is confirmed. Sign in and open your dashboard to download your PDF guides or start the test engine. You also receive an email confirmation.

The test engine works in a modern web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. PDF guides open on any device with a PDF reader.
